Safety rules

  • Swap drivers every 2.5 to 3 hours, even if the driver feels fine.
  • At night the front passenger stays awake and keeps the driver talking.
  • Refuel at half a tank on rural Mississippi stretches. Many stations close overnight.
  • Watch for deer on US 49 and US 98, especially between dusk and dawn. Use high beams when the road is clear.
  • Possible toll at the Mid Bay Bridge into Destin. Have a card or toll tag ready.
  • No one drives two legs in a row. Use the auto assign button to keep the rotation fair.

Pins are approximate. Tap a pin for the name, day, and a directions link. The route line is a simplified sketch of I-20, US 49, US 98, I-10, and FL 85.

Rain backup plan

  • Gulfarium Marine Adventure Park on Okaloosa Island. Plenty of indoor exhibits.
  • Fat Daddy's Arcade for games and a few hours out of the rain.
  • Destin Commons for covered shopping, a movie, and food.
  • A long seafood lunch on the harbor at HarborWalk Village, watching the boats come in.
